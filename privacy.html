Sparkcue · @getsparkcue

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Sparkcue is a social media channel that publishes short AI tips, presented by Bit, an animated robot. This policy explains what information the Sparkcue app handles when it publishes our posts and talks with our audience on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

1What we collect

The Sparkcue app publishes content to accounts we own. It does not ask you to sign in and does not collect information from your device. When you interact with our posts, we may process:

  • Comments on our posts, with your public username, so we can read and reply to them.
  • Messages you send to our accounts, so we can answer you, for example by sending a requested guide.
  • Aggregate statistics the platforms provide about our own posts, such as views and likes.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contact lists or location.

2How we use it

  • To reply to comments and messages.
  • To understand which topics our audience finds useful.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with data brokers.

3Services we rely on

Our content and replies pass through these providers, each under its own privacy policy: Meta (Instagram and Facebook), Google (YouTube), TikTok, Anthropic (AI used to research and write posts and replies), ElevenLabs (Bit's voice) and Telegram (our internal approval tool). When a reply is written with AI, the text of the comment or message may be sent to Anthropic only to produce that reply.

Our app uses YouTube API Services to publish videos to our own channel. By watching our content on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access our app has to our own Google account can be revoked at Google security settings.

4How we protect it

Access keys are stored only on our private server, readable only by the app, and never published or shared. Connections to every platform use encrypted HTTPS. The app accepts instructions only from the Sparkcue owner.

5How long we keep it

Comment and message text processed by our app is kept for no more than 90 days, then deleted. Comments stay visible on the platform until you or we remove them there.

6Deleting your data

To ask us to delete any information our app holds about you, email us with your username and the platform (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or TikTok). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Email: sparkcue1@gmail.com

You can also delete your own comments at any time on the platform, and remove the Sparkcue app from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites if you connected it.

7Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what information our app holds about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us (section 10) and we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, we process comments and messages based on our legitimate interest in replying to our audience, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8Children

Sparkcue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their information. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the information.

9Changes

If this policy changes, we update it on this page and change the effective date above.
