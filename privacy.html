Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Sparkcue (@getsparkcue) publishes short AI tips presented by Bit, an AI-made robot character, on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok, and runs a free email course, a newsletter and a Discord community. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and what you can do about it.

Sparkcue is run by Orel Dahan, an individual based in Israel, who is the controller of the information described here ("we", "us"). Contact: sparkcue1@gmail.com.

2How we use it, and our legal bases

  • Emails: your consent, given when you sign up and confirm. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Replies on Discord and social platforms: our legitimate interest in answering the questions you send us.
  • Security and moderation: our legitimate interest in keeping the community and the website safe.

We don't sell or "share" personal information (as defined by California law), build advertising profiles, or pass data to data brokers. Our website doesn't track you across sites, so it doesn't respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

  • Kit (email delivery and subscriber list, USA)
  • Anthropic (AI that writes Bit's answers and helps research posts, USA)

Some of these providers are in the USA. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4YouTube and Google data

Our app uses YouTube API Services, and accesses only our own YouTube channel (scope youtube) to upload our videos and read the channel name. It does not access viewers' Google data. Sparkcue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The access token is stored only on our private server and deleted when we disconnect.

By watching our content on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access to a Google account can be revoked at Google security settings.

5TikTok

With TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, our app signs in only to the Sparkcue TikTok account, to upload our own videos and read that account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar). The access token stays on our private server, is used only to post our own content, and is deleted when we disconnect. We don't collect data about TikTok viewers beyond public comments on our posts. Access can be revoked in TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

6How we protect it

Access keys are stored only on our private server, readable only by the app, and never published or shared. Connections to every service use encrypted HTTPS. The app accepts instructions only from the Sparkcue owner, and nothing is published without the owner's approval.

7How long we keep it

  • Comment, message and Discord question text: up to 90 days.
  • Email address and course day: until you unsubscribe; then removed from our records within 30 days. Kit may keep a record that you unsubscribed so you are not emailed again.
  • Access tokens: until we disconnect the account.

Comments stay visible on each platform until you or we remove them there.

8Deleting your data

To delete what we hold about you, email us with your username and where you used Sparkcue (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Discord or email). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. On Instagram or Facebook you can also send us a direct message with the word DELETE.

Email: sparkcue1@gmail.com

You can also delete your own comments and Discord messages yourself at any time, and unsubscribe from emails with the link at the bottom of every email.

9Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email us and we answer within 30 days. Under Israel's Privacy Protection Law you have the right to review and correct information about you, and you may complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10Children

Sparkcue is for people 13 and older (16 in EU and UK countries where local law requires parental consent below that age). Our email list and Discord are not for anyone younger. If we learn we have information from a child under 13, we delete it.

11Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes are announced in Bit's Weekly.
