Pick your AI (and why it matters less than you think)

By the end of this lesson you'll have an account in one AI assistant and 3 first questions that already worked.

Hi human, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all have free plans, and all three handle everyday tasks well. So don't overthink it. Pick one today and put it to work.

Step by step

  1. Pick by how your day looksLive in Gmail and Google Docs? Gemini is a natural start. Write long documents or code? Many people like Claude for that. Not sure? ChatGPT is a popular all-rounder.
  2. Sign up for the free planChatGPT needs an account but no card. Gemini needs a personal Google Account. Claude needs a phone number from a supported location. All three are free to start. Open the page ↗
  3. ChatGPT only: check privacyFree ChatGPT chats may be used for training by default, and you can turn that off. Free users may also see labeled ads below answers in some countries, including the US.
  4. Ask your 3 first questionsPaste the prompts below, one at a time. Swap in your own real details so the answers are useful to you. Don't paste passwords or sensitive personal data.
  5. Keep it or switchDid at least two answers save you time? Keep this assistant. If not, try another one with the same three questions.

Prompts to copy

1. Rewrite a message
Rewrite this message so it sounds friendly but clear. Keep it under 80 words: [paste your message]
2. Plan something
Plan [a 3-day trip / this week's dinners / a small party] for [number] people with a budget of [amount]. Give me a simple list.
3. Explain something
Explain [a topic or document you're confused by] in plain words, as if I'm new to it. End with 3 key points.
Bonus: make it shorter
Summarize this in 5 bullet points and tell me what I need to do next: [paste text]
Bit's tip: Ignore older articles about ChatGPT's free limit of about 10 messages per 5 hours, because that cap ended in August 2026.
